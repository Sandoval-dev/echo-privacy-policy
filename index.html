This policy concerns Echo Heist for Android (com.erdstudio.echoheist). The intended audience is ages 13 and older; the game is not directed to children under 13. Local digital-consent age rules may still apply.

Local game data Progress, stars, gold, lives, costumes, support inventory and language/audio preferences are stored on your device. The current game has no cloud save service or player-profile server. Clearing app data may erase this local save. Advertising and payment data are separate from local game progress.

Advertising Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform are used for advertising and applicable consent flows. Google's advertising SDK may process/share IP addresses (which can be used for approximate location), device/advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information for ads, measurement and fraud prevention. A request for non-personalized ads does not eliminate all technical data processing. Consent must be obtained before ad requests where required. Where available, Settings → Ad privacy options lets you manage your choices. Android settings also provide advertising-ID controls.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases Planned production service; real billing is currently disabled. Google Play will process payments when optional paid packs become available. Erd Studio does not receive your card number or Google password. A trusted verification service will process product/package identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase status and receipt records needed to prevent duplicate rewards. The prepared Firebase service requires pseudonymous authentication and app verification; the client integration is not yet connected. Update this policy with the actual identity and deletion flow before activation.

Support, security and retention If you email support, we process your email address, message and any diagnostics you choose to provide to answer the request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or service-account keys.

Network services use HTTPS/TLS. Support and transaction-verification records should be retained only as needed to provide the service, prevent duplicate fulfillment/fraud, resolve disputes and meet applicable retention obligations. A concrete server retention/deletion schedule must be implemented before launch.

Contact osmankilic10@gmail.com for access, correction or deletion requests. We may need to verify that the request relates to the record. Retention exceptions required by law or fraud prevention must be explained. Google's independently processed data is also subject to Google's own request mechanisms. Applicable local rights remain available. Service providers may process data outside your country; finalize transfer disclosures against the deployed services and target markets.

The policy and its date will be updated if services or practices change. Significant changes will be communicated in-app when required.
